Welcome to PasteCheck
PasteCheck watches what you copy and warns you before you paste a secret. Everything stays on this Mac. Three things make it work.
Allow clipboard access
macOS will ask when PasteCheck first reads the clipboard. Allow it there, or in System Settings under Privacy & Security, so checks can run.
Allow notifications
Warnings arrive as notifications. They name the kind of secret and where it sits, never the value itself.
Link SecChain (optional)
Warn when your own saved secrets are copied, not just look-alikes. Values never leave SecChain.
